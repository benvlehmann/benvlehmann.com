Bibliography

Editions of Spinoza

  • B. d. S. Opera posthuma, quorum series post praefationem exhibetur. [Amsterdam: Jan Rieuwertsz], 1677. Cited as OP; its list of errata is the Sphalmata corrigenda.
  • De Nagelate Schriften van B. d. S. [Amsterdam: Rieuwertsz], 1677. The Dutch translation published with the Opera posthuma; cited as NS, or as “the Dutch version”.
  • Paulus, H. E. G. (ed.). Opera quae supersunt omnia. 2 vols. Jena, 1802–1803.
  • Gfroerer, A. (ed.). Opera philosophica omnia. Stuttgart, 1830.
  • Bruder, C. H. (ed.). Opera quae supersunt omnia. 3 vols. Leipzig: Tauchnitz, 1843–1846.
  • Van Vloten, J. (ed.). Ad Benedicti de Spinoza opera quae supersunt omnia supplementum, continens tractatum hucusque ineditum de Deo et homine, tractatulum de iride, epistolas nonnullas ineditas, et ad eas vitamque philosophi collectanea. Amsterdam: Frederik Muller, 1862.
  • Ginsberg, H. (ed.). Die Ethik des Spinoza im Urtexte. Leipzig and Heidelberg: Weiss, 1875 [1876].
  • Van Vloten, J., and J. P. N. Land (eds.). Opera quotquot reperta sunt. The Hague: Nijhoff, 1882; 2nd ed., 3 vols., 1895 (cited as VVL²); 3rd ed., 4 vols., 1914 (cited as VVL³).
  • Gentile, G. (ed.). Ethica. Bari: Laterza, 1915.
  • Gebhardt, Carl (ed.). Spinoza Opera, im Auftrag der Heidelberger Akademie der Wissenschaften herausgegeben. 4 vols. Heidelberg: Carl Winter, 1925. Cited as G, by volume and page; Gebhardt’s account of the text in each volume is cited as Textgestaltung. Spinoza’s other works and his letters are cited from this edition.
  • Bartuschat, Wolfgang (rev.). Baruch de Spinoza, Opera. Nach der von Carl Gebhardt besorgten Ausgabe von 1925, vols. I–IV, with the Ethica, the Tractatus politicus and the Tractatus de intellectus emendatione revised by Wolfgang Bartuschat. Digital edition, without place or date.
  • Meijer, Rudolf W. (transcr.). Ethica ordine geometrico demonstrata. Transcription of the text of 1677 in classical orthography, encoded by Ph. Roelli for the Corpus Corporum, University of Zurich, 2013 (item 12182). The Latin text of this edition.

Translations of the Ethics

  • Auerbach. German translation, 1841, based on that of Valentin Schmidt.
  • Saisset, E. French translation, 1842.
  • Elwes, R. H. M. (tr.). The Chief Works of Benedict de Spinoza. 2 vols. London: George Bell (Bohn’s Philosophical Library), 1883–1884. The Ethics is in vol. II (1884), cited by the pages of that volume, which the revised printing of 1891 keeps.
  • White, W. Hale (tr.). Ethic, Demonstrated in Geometrical Order and Divided into Five Parts. London: Trübner, 1883 (The English and Foreign Philosophical Library, vol. XXI). Revised by Miss Stirling, as White’s preface says.
  • White, W. Hale (tr.). Ethic, Demonstrated in Geometrical Order and Divided into Five Parts. Translation revised by Amelia Hutchison Stirling. 4th ed. London: Humphrey Milford / Oxford University Press, 1910. First published 1883.
  • Meijer, Willem (tr.). Dutch translation. Amsterdam: van Looy, 1896; 2nd ed. 1905.
  • Baensch, Otto (tr.). German translation. Leipzig: Dürr, 1904; 2nd ed. Leipzig: Meiner, 1910.
  • Lantzenberg. French translation. Paris: Flammarion, 1908.
  • Appuhn, Ch. (tr.). French translation, with the Latin text. Paris: Garnier, 1909.
  • Halpern, J. (tr.). Polish translation of Spinoza’s works. Warszawa: Gebethner i Wolff, 1914.

Authors quoted at second hand are cited with the work that quotes them.
