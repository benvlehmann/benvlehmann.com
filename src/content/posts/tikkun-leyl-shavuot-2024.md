I often have conversations with religiously-inclined folks who want my take on how to understand the creation narrative in Judaism. I'd like to think that I have some perspective on this point: in addition to being a practicing cosmologist, I have a fairly traditional Jewish background, and I've spent a good deal of time studying Jewish texts.

Eventually, I decided to write some of my thoughts down, and I took the opportunity to share them in a shiur (lesson) at a Tikkun Leyl Shavuot at Harvard Hillel in 2024. There is a tradition of teaching and studying Jewish texts all night on the Shavuot holiday, and in Cambridge, people tend to bring in adjacent perspectives from their professional lives.

The four-page source sheet as printed for the shiur can be found [here](../../s/sources.pdf).

An extensive 60-page summary of the topics in the shiur, including both the cosmology and the textual aspects, can be found [here](../../s/bereishit_cosmology.pdf).
